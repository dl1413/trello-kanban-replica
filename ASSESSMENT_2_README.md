- Deep understanding of state management
- Attention to UI/UX details
- Knowledge of design systems

**How to Demonstrate**:
- Show pixel-perfect replication
- Explain CSS challenges solved
- Demonstrate hover/active states
- Discuss design token extraction

### 2. Velocity & Efficiency Mindset (25%)
- Proactive use of tools and automation
- Evidence of rapid development
- Smart workflow optimizations
- Time-saving techniques

**How to Demonstrate**:
- Discuss AI-assisted development
- Show time breakdown
- Explain automation tools
- Share non-conventional methods

- Context-appropriate decisions
- Understanding of tradeoffs
- Knowledge of alternatives
- Balanced approach to complexity

**How to Demonstrate**:

- Discuss tradeoffs made

- Full-stack thinking
- Production readiness awareness

- Discuss scalability plans

## 💡 Key Success Factors

### Do These Things:
✅ **Be Yourself**: Authenticity beats memorization
✅ **Show Enthusiasm**: Passion for your work is contagious
✅ **Think Aloud**: Explain your thought process
✅ **Ask Questions**: Clarify when unsure
✅ **Admit Limitations**: Honesty builds trust
✅ **Stay Positive**: Frame challenges as learning opportunities

### Avoid These Pitfalls:
❌ **Don't Memorize Word-for-Word**: Sound natural, not robotic
❌ **Don't Be Defensive**: Accept feedback graciously
❌ **Don't Ramble**: Be concise and structured
❌ **Don't Pretend**: Say "I don't know" when true
❌ **Don't Rush**: Take time to think before answering
❌ **Don't Panic**: Mistakes are okay, recovery matters

## 📈 Scoring Rubric

### Visual Fidelity (0-2 points)
- 0: Significant discrepancies
- 1: Minor issues
- 2: Pixel-perfect ✨

### Functional Accuracy (0-2 points)
- 0: Broken/missing
- 1: Present but glitchy
- 2: Smooth and accurate ✨

### Workflow Efficiency (0-2 points)
- 0: No automation
- 1: Standard tools
- 2: Advanced automation ✨

### Code Structure (0-2 points)
- 0: Disorganized
- 1: Clear separation
- 2: Production-ready ✨

### Commitment to Detail (0-2 points)
- 0: Ignored elements
- 1: Handled major elements
- 2: Every detail perfect ✨

**Target Score**: 7-10 out of 10
**Passing Threshold**: 7
